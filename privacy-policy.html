Detective on Duty — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026 · Quup Games

Detective on Duty is a detective game. You do not need an account, an email address or any identity document to play. This page sets out, in full, what the game does with data.

Your save stays on your device

Your rank, the files you have closed, your diamonds and all of your progress are stored in your phone's own storage. None of it is sent to a server. Removing the app removes the save.

The one thing that goes to a server: the weekly leaderboard

The game has a weekly ranking. To make it work, one row of data per week is kept on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore):

DataWhat it is
Player handleThe nickname you chose in the game. Not your real name.
Weekly scoreThe experience you earned that week.
RankYour level in the game.
Avatar numberThe index of the stock character face you picked.
Anonymous IDA random number assigned to your device. It does not identify you.

Sign-in is anonymous: Firebase gives your device a random identifier that is not tied to your name, email or phone number. Other players can see this row on the ranking screen — that is what it is for.

Analytics and advertising

ServicePurposeWhat it processes
Google Firebase Analytics Understanding which screens are used and where the game gets difficult Device model, OS version, country, in-app events
Google AdMob Showing the adverts in the game Advertising ID, ad impression and click data, approximate (country-level) location
Google Firebase Remote Config Adjusting game balance without shipping an update No personal data

These are Google services and are subject to Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings.

Purchases

You can buy diamonds in the game. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. Your card details, your name and your billing information never reach us; Google only tells us that a purchase completed.

What we do not collect

Name, surname, email, phone number, address, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, payment details. The game asks for none of these permissions.

Deleting your data

In the game, go to Settings → Delete Account. This erases the save on your device and removes your row from the weekly leaderboard on the server. It cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the app also removes the save on the device, but your leaderboard row may remain until the end of that week. For permanent removal, use "Delete Account" in the game first.

If you have a request about deleting your data, write to us at the address below.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
